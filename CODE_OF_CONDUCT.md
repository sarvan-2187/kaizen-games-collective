# Code of Conduct

We want Kaizen Games Collective to be a friendly place for makers and players of every background and experience level. This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Our standard

Good behaviour: being kind and patient, giving and accepting constructive feedback, crediting other people's work, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing other people's private information
- Submitting games or comments that are hateful, sexually explicit or graphically violent
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, issues, pull requests and games that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate or harmful.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers using GitHub's "Report content" option, or by opening a private security advisory titled "Conduct report" (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). All reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.
